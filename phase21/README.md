# Phase 21 — Iran-First Authority & Citation Network

## Mission

Turn verified Keshavarz20 first-party evidence into reusable citation assets without making completion depend on cold outreach, backlinks, or manufacturer replies.

## Hard rules

- Manufacturer replies, backlinks and Digital PR are optional accelerators, not acceptance gates.
- Generated Question Engine content is synthetic editorial material, not customer evidence.
- Candidate-only compatibility remains candidate-only.
- Unknown product facts remain unknown.
- Every citable claim needs a source reference, scope and limitation.
- Search/AI citation counts are never inferred from ordinary traffic.
- No PBN, fake mentions, fake reviews, fake dealership claims, fabricated field results or mass advertorials.

- `phase17/evidence-cards.json`
- `phase9-results/canonical-pim.json`
- `growth-os/intent-registry.json`
- `growthos-phase10-results/measurement-dashboard.json`
- `question-engine-results/20260921T094817Z-status.json`

## Outputs

- `phase21-results/authority-network-2026-09-27.json`
- `phase21-results/manufacturer-source-registry-2026-09-27.json`
- `phase21-results/question-evidence-classification-2026-09-27.json`

## Acceptance

Phase 21 passes when:
1. Grade-A first-party evidence exists.
2. PIM stable IDs and schema/feed parity are intact.
3. Hard fabrications are zero.
4. Unsupported compatibility is not promoted.
5. Canonical Intent Graph is active.
6. Persisted Search Console demand evidence exists.
7. Synthetic questions are prevented from being counted as customer evidence.
8. Manufacturer outreach is optional and cannot create endorsement claims.
